The Exact Social connect service keeps nothing about you after you connect: what Meta sends is deleted as soon as your WordPress site collects it, or after 10 minutes at most (see the privacy policy). Your access tokens and account details live only on the WordPress site you connected. To remove them:

1. Disconnect the account in Exact Social

In WordPress, open Exact Social → Social accounts and choose Disconnect on the account. Its access token is deleted from your site.

2. Remove the app in your Meta settings

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, then remove Exact Social.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, then remove Exact Social.
  • Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions, then remove Exact Social.

This stops every token the app was given from working, on any site.

3. Remove the rest from your WordPress site

In Exact Social → Settings, under This site, turn on "Delete all data when uninstalling" (Owners see it), then delete the plugin from Plugins. Its workspaces, posts, accounts and history are removed from your site's database.
