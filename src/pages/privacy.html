This policy covers the Exact Social connect service at connect.exactwp.com and the Exact Social app on Facebook, Instagram and Threads, run by Exact Studios. Questions and requests: info@exactwp.com.

What the connect service does

Exact Social is a WordPress plugin that schedules and publishes posts from your own WordPress site. To connect a Facebook Page, an Instagram account or a Threads account, the plugin sends you through this service, which signs you in with Meta and hands the result to your site. After that, your site talks to Meta directly; this service isn't involved in publishing.

What it receives while you connect

  • The address of the WordPress site that started the connect, so it can send you back.
  • The one-time code Meta sends after you sign in, which the service exchanges for access tokens.
  • From Meta: your account ID and name; for Facebook, the Pages you chose to share (ID, name, picture, your role on the Page, its access token) and any Instagram account linked to them (ID, username, name, picture); for Instagram and Threads, the account's ID, username, name and picture; and the access tokens that let your site publish for you.
  • A cookie, exs_relay, that ties the sign-in to your browser for up to 10 minutes, so nobody else can finish a connect you started.

How long it keeps it

The result is stored encrypted on the service's server under a one-time code, for at most 10 minutes. It's deleted as soon as your site collects it, or when the 10 minutes run out, whichever comes first. The service keeps no account, profile or token after that.

The server's logs record requests (time, IP address, browser, address visited) and connection failures as reported by Meta. They contain no access tokens.

What your WordPress site keeps

Your site stores the access tokens and account details it collected, encrypted, in its own database, and uses them to publish the posts you schedule. That data stays on your site: Exact Studios doesn't receive it or have access to it. Whoever runs the site controls it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share the data, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data.
  • The service's pages have no analytics or tracking.
  • We don't read your posts, messages or insights; the service only signs you in.

Your choices

You can disconnect an account in Exact Social at any time, and remove the app's access in your Facebook, Instagram or Threads settings. See deleting your data for the steps. For anything else about your data, write to info@exactwp.com.
